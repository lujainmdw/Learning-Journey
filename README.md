# Learning Journey
An iOS app that helps you stay consistent in your **learning journey** by encouraging daily check-ins to build and maintain a learning streak

## Key Features
### 🔥 streaks
**Log as learned** each day to build and maintain your daily learning streak

### ❄️ Freezes
Need a break? Use a **Freeze** to protect your streak on days you don't feel like learning. Freeze allowances scale with your goal duration
* **Weekly Goal:** 2 freezes
* **Monthly Goal:** 8 freezes
* **Yearly Goal:** 96 freezes

### ⚠️ You lose your streak when
* More than **32 hours** pass without *logging as Learned* or using a *Freeze*
* You update your Learning goal

### 🎨 Custom Theme Colors
Personalize the app's look and feel with a **theme color picker**, letting you switch between curated color presets that dynamically update buttons, highlights, and accents across the app

### 📱 Home Screen Widget
Keep your streak top-of-mind without even opening the app—view your current streak progress at a glance directly from your iOS Home Screen

### 🧪 Testing Button
Simulate the passage of time with a **debug/testing button** to fast-forward Days, making it easy to test streak updates, Freeze consumption, and the 32-hour expiration window without waiting in real time

## Built With
* **Language:** Swift
* **UI Framework:** SwiftUI
* **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
